Insight (InsightAI)

A personalized learning platform built with Justin Baratta to turn a learner’s prompt and difficulty preference into a structured course.

The idea

Insight is designed for learners who know what they want to study but cannot find a resource at the right depth. The app uses an LLM to create a course from a text prompt and a difficulty selector, then organizes that course into lessons with examples, sources, and practice problems.

My contribution

This was a collaboration between Logan Vimalaraj and Justin Baratta. The project combines product design, full-stack software, and applied machine learning into a single learning experience. The details on this page describe the project as it exists today; future updates will document new capabilities as they are released.

Recognition

Insight won the 2025 Congressional App Challenge for Nevada’s 2nd District. The official announcement was published in January 2026.
