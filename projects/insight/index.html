Insight

A personalized learning platform built with Justin Baratta. It turns a prompt and a difficulty setting into a structured course.

The idea

Insight is for learners who know what they want to study but can’t find a resource at the right depth. The app uses an LLM to build a course from a text prompt and a difficulty selector, then organizes it into lessons with examples, sources and practice problems.

The team

Insight was a collaboration between Logan Vimalaraj and Justin Baratta, combining product design, full-stack software and applied machine learning in one learning experience.

Recognition

Insight won the 2025 Congressional App Challenge for Nevada’s 2nd District. The official announcement was published in January 2026.
