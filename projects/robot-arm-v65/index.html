Robot Arm v6.5

A five-degree-of-freedom robot arm with a gripper, designed as part of the DUM-E home-assistant robot.

Design goals

Robot Arm v6.5 builds on the v6 foundation while moving more of the arm’s weight away from the shoulder. The design uses NEMA 17 motors for every degree of freedom other than the gripper and is intended to provide a larger, more reliable work area for DUM-E.

Transmission and wrist

A fiberglass-reinforced GT2 belt drives the elbow and adds a 3:1 pulley reduction. The wrist uses differential helical bevel gears, which allows its motors to be belt-driven while also acting as counterweights for the payload. The actuators reuse the stackable 5:1 planetary gearbox Logan designed for the v6 arm.

What comes next

This arm is a hardware platform for DUM-E’s software stack. Future updates will document calibration, control firmware, and the arm’s role in precise VLA-based manipulation as those parts are ready to share.
