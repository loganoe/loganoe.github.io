01 Flagship project

Hardware complete · Software in progress

DUM-E

A general-purpose home robot. Give it a long-horizon command like “clean this room” and it plans the steps, drives itself where it needs to be, and does the work with its arm.

Drivebase + vertical lift + 6-DOF arm
All three now built and working together.
Project page →

  1. 01

    A 6-DOF arm

    The arm is my Robot Arm v7: six degrees of freedom plus a gripper, custom cycloidal drives in the joints, and closed-loop control on the base, shoulder and elbow.

    Robot Arm v7 in detail

  2. 02

    A vertical lift

    The whole arm rides a stepper-driven carriage on an aluminium mast, so the same arm can work near the floor or reach up to desk height.

  3. 03

    A drivebase

    An aluminium-skinned mobile base carries the tower and its electronics around the house. The LLM planner and SLAM stack steer it directly.

  4. 04

    A brain

    A Raspberry Pi 4 inside the white base is the onboard computer and ROS 2 master, with an ESP32 bridging it to the motors. On top, an LLM + pointer + SLAM planner breaks a command into steps and a VLA model handles precise arm control.

Software stack

From one sentence to motor steps

  1. Input “Clean this room” Long-horizon text command
  2. Plan LLM + pointer Breaks the task into smaller steps
  3. Navigate SLAM Drives the base to each step
  4. Manipulate VLA policy Precise control of the arm
  5. Actuate ROS 2 → ESP32 Joint commands to the steppers

02 The manipulator

Project page Repo on GitHub ↗

Robot Arm v7

The seventh version of my arm, and the one DUM-E carries. I designed every part from scratch in Onshape.

Degrees of freedom
6+ gripper
Reach
~500mm
Payload
~800g
Cycloidal reduction
15: 1

Teleop Driven live by a leader arm I built around AS5600 magnetic encoders.

Specification

Wrist
RPR: a roll joint, then a differential for pitch and roll
Motors
NEMA 23 on the shoulder; NEMA 17 on the base, elbow and wrist
Feedback
Closed loop on the base, shoulder and elbow
Reduction
Custom low-profile cycloidal drives on the joints
Structure
2020 aluminium extrusion segments with 3D-printed parts
Gripper
DS3218MG servo
Control
ESP32 bridge to a ROS 2 master, such as a Raspberry Pi

The 15:1 cycloidal gearbox is open source. Download it on MakerWorld ↗

03 Lineage

Each arm taught the next one.

v3NEMA 17 · belt elbow

My first arm to use NEMA 17 steppers and a belt-driven elbow, with 3:1 planetary reductions (not my own design yet).

v4Custom actuators

The first arm with actuators I designed entirely myself. Every joint runs my 9:1 cycloidal drive.

v5800 mm reach

Aluminium extrusion segments made a huge 800 mm reach possible. A 64:1 three-stage planetary drives the shoulder and 9:1 cycloidals drive the elbow and base.

v625:1 stacked planetary

Smaller than v5 but with far more torque headroom. Stackable 5:1 planetary gearboxes, doubled up to 25:1 at the shoulder and base.

v6.5Differential wrist

5 DOF plus gripper. A fiberglass-reinforced GT2 belt drives the elbow, and the wrist uses differential helical bevel gears. Read more

v7Now on DUM-E

6 DOF plus gripper with custom cycloidal drives and closed-loop joints. It’s the arm that rides on DUM-E. Back to v7
